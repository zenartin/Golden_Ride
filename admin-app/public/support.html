Frequently Asked Questions

For Riders:

Q: How do I request a ride?
A: Open the Golden Ride app, input your destination, choose a vehicle class, and tap "Request Ride".

Q: How do I change my payment method?
A: Navigate to the Account/Payment tab within your user application settings to update or select a different payment card.

For Drivers:

Q: How long does document verification take?
A: Driver profile and document verification is typically processed by our administration team within 24 hours.

Q: When are my payouts sent?
A: Weekly earnings are processed and transferred automatically every Monday morning.
