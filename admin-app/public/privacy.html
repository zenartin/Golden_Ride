Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 8, 2026

At Golden Ride, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use our Golden Ride (User App) and Golden Ride Driver applications, and our associated services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Profile Information: Name, email address, phone number, and profile picture.
  • Location Data: Real-time location information to match riders with drivers and track ride progress.
  • Payment Information: Transaction and payment processing details.
  • Device Information: IP address, device type, and operating system.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To facilitate ride-matching and navigation.
  • To process payments securely.
  • To provide customer support and resolve disputes.
  • To ensure the safety and security of our users and drivers.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information with:

  • Riders and drivers to coordinate active trips.
  • Payment processors and third-party service providers acting on our behalf.
  • Law enforcement agencies when required by applicable law.

4. Your Privacy Choices

You can access and update your account information directly in the application settings. You may also disable location sharing, though doing so will limit the application's core functionality.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@golden-ride.com.
